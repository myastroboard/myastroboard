# Quick Start

From a fresh installation to your first night plan. Each step links to the page that covers it in
full.

## 1. Sign in and change the default password

Open `http://<host>:5000` and sign in with `admin` / `admin`. A warning reminds you to change
that password at each sign-in until it is done (My settings -> Security). See [AUTHENTICATION.md](AUTHENTICATION.md).

## 2. Follow the setup wizard

On a fresh install the admin account opens a guided wizard: **location**, **sky quality**
(Bortle / SQM), **equipment**, **notifications**, then a preview of tonight's best targets. Every
step can be skipped and done later. See
[BEGINNER_EXPERIENCE.md](BEGINNER_EXPERIENCE.md#guided-setup-wizard).

What the wizard fills in can be changed afterwards:

| Setting | Where |
|---|---|
| Location, timezone, sky quality, horizon profile | App settings -> Locations ([LOCATIONS.md](LOCATIONS.md)) |
| SkyTonight constraints (altitude, airmass, size, Moon separation) | App settings -> Configuration ([CONFIGURATION.md](CONFIGURATION.md)) |
| Telescopes, cameras, combinations | Equipment ([EQUIPMENT.md](EQUIPMENT.md)) |
| Notifications | My settings -> Notifications ([NOTIFICATIONS.md](NOTIFICATIONS.md)) |
| Theme, language, startup tab, experience level | My settings -> Customize ([USER_SETTINGS.md](USER_SETTINGS.md)) |

## 3. Wait for the first SkyTonight run

SkyTonight computes tonight's targets in the background: once at startup, then one hour after
nautical dawn and one hour before nautical dusk. The first run takes a few minutes; its progress
shows in the SkyTonight tab. To rerun it after changing a setting, use **Run SkyTonight Now** in
App settings -> Configuration. See [SKYTONIGHT.md](SKYTONIGHT.md).

## 4. Decide whether tonight is worth it

- **Astrophotography** tab: the night's score, the best imaging window, the Moon, the Sun, aurora
  and upcoming events ([ASTROPHOTOGRAPHY.md](ASTROPHOTOGRAPHY.md)).
- **Weather** tab: hourly forecast, seeing and transparency, observation conditions
  ([WEATHER.md](WEATHER.md)).

## 5. Pick targets and plan the night

1. In **SkyTonight -> Deep Sky Objects**, sort by AstroScore, filter by type or constellation, and
   open a target for its altitude chart and details.
2. Add targets to **Plan My Night** (Astrodex -> Plan my night), set a duration for each, and let
   the optimizer order them by visibility ([PLAN_MY_NIGHT.md](PLAN_MY_NIGHT.md)).
3. Check the framing of a target with your camera in the framing simulator
   ([SKYTONIGHT.md](SKYTONIGHT.md#framing-simulator)).

## 6. After the night

- Turn the plan into an **Observation Log** session and record what you actually shot
  ([OBSERVATION_LOG.md](OBSERVATION_LOG.md)).
- Add your pictures to the **Astrodex**, your personal collection of imaged objects
  ([ASTRODEX.md](ASTRODEX.md)).
- Watch your progress in **Your Sky** and keep a **Wishlist** of what is next
  ([SESSION_ANALYTICS.md](SESSION_ANALYTICS.md)).
