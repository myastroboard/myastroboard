- `backend/utils/auth.py`: `TOTP` is imported from `pyotp.totp`. pyotp 2.12 added `py.typed`, but
  its `__init__` re-exports `TOTP` only implicitly, so pyright reported two
  `reportPrivateImportUsage` errors. No behavior change. No test was modified.
